How to paste into Google Sheet

1
Open your FinTrack Google Sheet → click the Transactions tab at the bottom
2
Click on the first empty cell in column A (below the last existing row, or row 2 if empty)
3
Press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac) to paste. Choose "Split text to columns" if prompted — select Comma as separator
4
Go back to the FinTrack app → tap the ↻ sync button (top right) — your transactions will load instantly
⚠️ Duplicate check: If you're importing the same statement twice, the Sheet may get duplicate rows. Always check the Sheet before pasting to avoid counting transactions twice.
